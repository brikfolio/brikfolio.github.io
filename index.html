FAQ

Questions investors ask us

What is Brikfolio?

Brikfolio is an AI-powered property intelligence platform for Australian property investors. It brings your portfolio, debt, tax and documents into one view, then flags the moments to act — such as a refinance window, a lease expiry or an end-of-financial-year CGT decision.

Who is Brikfolio for?

Brikfolio is built for Australian residential property investors — from investors with one or two properties to those managing a multi-property portfolio across several banks and property managers.

How much does Brikfolio cost?

There is a Free plan for up to 2 properties. Pro is $30 per month billed yearly or $50 billed monthly. Elite is $60 per month billed yearly or $100 billed monthly. All prices are in Australian dollars.

When can I start using Brikfolio?

Brikfolio is in pre-launch. Join the waitlist and you will be among the first invited when early access opens.

Can I share my portfolio with my accountant or advisor?

Yes. The Pro and Elite plans let you share access with your advisors, accountants and agents.

How is my data protected?

Brikfolio uses safeguards including encrypted transmission, access controls, role-based permissions, audit logs, backups and security monitoring. Read the Security Policy Summary.

Is Brikfolio financial advice?

No. Brikfolio provides software tools only. It does not provide financial, investment, tax, lending or legal advice. Speak to a licensed professional before you act on any decision.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. You can cancel in your account settings or by emailing support@brikfolio.com.au. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.
